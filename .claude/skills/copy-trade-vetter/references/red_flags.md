# Copy-trading red flags — full checklist

Each item: what to look for, why it matters, and how severe it usually is (🔴 fatal on its own, 🟡 caution, stacks with others).

## Track record integrity

- 🔴 **Equity curve predates the verified/join date.** Anything before verification could be imported, cherry-picked, or from another account. Judge only the verified portion.
- 🟡 **Short history (< 12 months).** One market regime; a trend-following or carry strategy can look brilliant for a year and then give it all back.
- 🟡 **Few trades (< 200).** Too small a sample to separate skill from luck.
- 🔴 **Demo account** or "hypothetical"/backtested results presented alongside live ones.
- 🟡 **Gaps, resets, or deposits that coincide with drawdowns.** Topping up a losing account flatters percentage returns.
- 🟡 **"Audit" or "verified" badges that aren't actually ticked** for this specific strategy (e.g., a CPA-audit column showing ✗).

## Blow-up risk signatures

- 🔴 **Martingale/grid:** lot size increases after losses; many open positions in the same instrument at stepped prices; very high win rate with rare, huge losses.
- 🔴 **No stop-losses / holding losers:** average loss ≫ average win (roughly 3× or more) together with win rate > 75%. Payoff looks like picking up pennies in front of a steamroller.
- 🔴 **Equity well below balance (> ~5%).** Losing positions are being carried open; realized stats (win rate, drawdown on balance) look better than reality.
- 🟡 **Staircase-smooth equity curve.** Real edges are bumpy. Unusually smooth curves are more often hidden tail risk than genius.
- 🟡 **Sharpe > 3 sustained** on a retail FX/CFD account — possible, but rare enough to demand extra proof.

## Return vs risk sanity

- 🟡 **Return/max-DD ratio > ~10** over a short period. Check whether drawdown is balance-based (hides floating loss) or equity-based.
- 🟡 **Avg monthly return > 5%** sustained. Compounds to >80%/yr; the best hedge funds don't do this. Usually means high leverage or tail risk.
- 🟡 **One or two months drive most of the total return.** Luck concentration.

## Selection & platform effects

- 🟡 **Survivorship bias.** Leaderboards show survivors; the strategies that blew up were delisted. Being #1 among thousands is expected by chance alone.
- 🟡 **Proprietary scores** ("Alpha Score", "AI Score"): opaque, backward-looking, often reward smoothness (which rewards martingales).
- 🟡 **Copier slippage.** Copies fill later and worse. Scalpers (avg hold < 15 min), news traders, and illiquid instruments degrade most.
- 🟡 **Capacity.** A strategy that worked on $50K may not work with many copiers piling into the same small-cap or exotic pair.

## Broker & counterparty

- 🔴 **Unregulated or offshore broker with no tier-1 license** (FCA, ASIC, CySEC, NFA/CFTC, etc.) holding the user's money. Withdrawal risk trumps strategy quality.
- 🟡 **Prop-firm/funded account results.** Often simulated capital; payout rules differ from real-money trading.
- 🟡 **US users:** many offshore copy platforms and CFD brokers can't legally serve US residents; check before signing up.

## Incentives & marketing

- 🟡 **Affiliate/referral link, IB rebates, or paid group upsell** in the pitch. The promoter earns on sign-ups or volume, not on the user's profits.
- 🟡 **Urgency/scarcity cues** ("save this before it gets taken down", "unethical", "nobody wants you to know").
- 🔴 **Asked to send money to a person/wallet** rather than a regulated broker account, or promised guaranteed returns. Classic scam pattern.
