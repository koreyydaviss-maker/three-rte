## How to mirror manually, if you choose to
1. **You place every trade yourself, on your own broker.** Nothing here is an instruction to buy.
2. **Paper trade first.** Or use a very small test amount, and only after a trader is rated "Small test allocation", which none are today.
3. **Size from drawdown, not from return** (skill rule). Plan for a future max drawdown of at least **2× the historical max**. Allocation = the dollar loss you can stomach ÷ (2 × historical max DD). No max drawdown is confirmed for thomaspj, CPHequities or JeppeKirkBonde, so **this can't be sized yet.** That alone is a reason to wait.
4. **Set a stop** on each position, and a total loss limit for the whole mirror, at the dollar loss you chose in step 3.
5. **Expect worse results than the trader.** You'll enter late and at worse prices, and you'll miss his trims and exits until they become public. Data 8–10 months old, like most of this page, is not a signal to trade.
6. Never use borrowed money or money you need within a year. Review monthly.
