## How to mirror manually, if you choose to
1. Use eToro CopyTrader on a Small-test trader rather than buying the list by hand.
2. Size each copy as your loss limit ÷ (2 × max drawdown). For a $500 limit, that's about $960 for Jeppe and about $750 for CPH.
3. Set the copy stop-loss at that loss limit, and review the copy monthly.
4. Try a practice account first if you're unsure.
